    How this is made

    The ATIS radio broadcast is recorded and turned into text by a computer every few minutes. The broadcast repeats, so it is transcribed several times and a value is shown only when the repetitions agree. Items heard only once are shown in orange; items that could not be heard clearly are left blank.
